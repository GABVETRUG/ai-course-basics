# AI Basics: Artificial Intelligence Tools for Everyone

## Course Program

---

### General Information

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Title** | AI Basics: Artificial Intelligence Tools for Everyone |
| **Duration** | 20 hours |
| **Format** | 10 sessions of 2h or 5 full days of 4h |
| **Theory/Practice ratio** | 35% theory — 65% practice |
| **Audience** | Non-technical staff, juniors, professionals who want to integrate AI into their work |
| **Prerequisites** | Basic computer and Internet skills. No programming knowledge required |
| **Materials provided** | PPT slides, in-depth PDF handouts, exercise sheets for each module |
| **Assessment** | Final project (25 points) + 20-question quiz (60% pass threshold) |
| **Certification** | Certificate of completion with certified competencies |

---

### Learning Objectives

By the end of the course, participants will be able to:

1. Understand what Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and LLMs are
2. Write effective prompts using structured prompt engineering techniques
3. Use ChatGPT, Claude, and open-source models proficiently
4. Choose the right AI tool based on the type of task
5. Integrate AI into daily work workflows (emails, reports, analysis, decision-making)
6. Apply responsible AI principles: privacy, bias, hallucinations, regulations
7. Create a corporate policy for AI usage

### Detailed Structure

#### Module 1 — What Is Artificial Intelligence (2 hours)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Theory | 1.5h |
| Practice | 0.5h |

**Theoretical Content:**
- Definition of AI, brief history (from Turing to ChatGPT)
- AI in everyday life: examples we already use
- Narrow AI vs General AI
- Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and LLMs: the three levels
- How an LLM works: next-word prediction
- Tokens, context window, temperature
- LLM limitations: what they CANNOT do
- Tools overview: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, open-source models

**Lab:**
- Account creation (ChatGPT, Claude, HuggingChat)
- Comparative exercise: same question on 3 tools
- Analysis of differences and discussion

**Materials:**
- Slides: 36 | PDF: AI Glossary (~35 terms) | Exercises: 3 activities + 5-question quiz

---

#### Module 2 — Prompt Engineering: The Art of Asking Well (3 hours)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Theory | 1h |
| Practice | 2h |

**Theoretical Content:**
- What is prompt engineering and why it is essential
- Anatomy of a good prompt: role, context, task, format, tone, constraints
- Techniques: zero-shot, few-shot, chain-of-thought, role-playing
- Structured output, decomposition, constraints, iteration
- Common mistakes and how to avoid them

**Lab:**
- Lab 1 (30 min): transform vague prompts into effective prompts (3 guided exercises)
- Lab 2 (45 min): 8 real-world work scenarios (brainstorming, reports, translation, social media, etc.)
- Lab 3 (30 min): tool comparison on the same prompt

**Materials:**
- Slides: 39 | PDF: Prompt Toolkit (20 ready-to-use templates) | Exercises: 3 labs + quiz

---

#### Module 3 — ChatGPT: Mastering the Most Widely Used Tool (3 hours)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Theory | 0.5h |
| Practice | 2.5h |

**Theoretical Content:**
- OpenAI and ChatGPT: history, models (GPT-4o, o3), plans and pricing
- Interface: navigating the app
- Features: chat, file upload, web browsing, DALL-E, Code Interpreter, Canvas
- Memory and custom instructions
- GPTs: what they are, GPT Store, creating a custom one

**Lab:**
- Lab 1 (25 min): executive summary from an uploaded document
- Lab 2 (30 min): data analysis with Code Interpreter (charts, insights, reports)
- Lab 3 (25 min): image generation with DALL-E
- Lab 4 (30 min): create a custom GPT for your own work

**Materials:**
- Slides: 40 | PDF: ChatGPT Cheat Sheet | Exercises: 4 labs + 5-question quiz

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Theory | 0.5h |
| Practice | 2.5h |

|--------|---------|
| Theory | 1h |
