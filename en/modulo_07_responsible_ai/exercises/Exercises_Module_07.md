## Exercise 3: Create your company AI policy (15 min)

### Instructions
In groups of 2-3 people, create a policy for AI use in your organization (real or fictional).

### Step 1: Define the context

**Company name**: _______________________________________________

**Industry**: ___________________________________________________

**Number of employees**: __________________________________________

**Departments that use/will use AI**: ______________________________

### Step 2: Ask the AI for a draft

Use this prompt (adapt it to your context):
```
Create a company policy for the use of generative AI
in a [INDUSTRY] company with [N] employees.

The policy should cover the following sections:
1. Purpose and scope
2. Authorized AI tools
3. Prohibited data and information
4. Mandatory human verification of output
5. Transparency and disclosure
6. Mandatory training
7. Liability
8. Review and updates

Requirements:
- Formal but understandable tone
- Length: max 2 pages
- Include practical examples for each section
- Adapt to the applicable regulatory framework (GDPR, EU AI Act)
```

### Step 3: Review and customize

After receiving the draft, verify and customize:

- [ ] **Tools**: is the list of authorized tools correct for our situation?
- [ ] **Prohibited data**: is the list complete? Is anything specific to our industry missing?
- [ ] **Output verification**: is the review process realistic and feasible?
- [ ] **Transparency**: are the disclosure rules clear?
- [ ] **Training**: are the training requirements reasonable?
- [ ] **Liability**: is it clear who is responsible in case of errors?
- [ ] **Language**: is it understandable for ALL employees, not just technical staff?

### Step 4: Group additions

Elements you want to add or modify compared to the AI draft:

```
1. ____________________________________________________________
2. ____________________________________________________________
3. ____________________________________________________________
```

### Step 5: Presentation (5 min per group)

Prepare a 5-minute presentation covering:
- Your company's context
- The 3 most important points of your policy
- The most surprising thing you discovered while creating it
- An open question for the group
