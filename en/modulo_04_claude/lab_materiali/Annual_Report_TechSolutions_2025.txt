1. LETTER FROM THE CEO
================================================================

Dear stakeholders,

2025 was a year of transformation for TechSolutions. In a
macroeconomic context characterized by geopolitical uncertainty and
rapid technological evolution, our company was able to seize the
opportunities offered by Artificial Intelligence and cloud
computing, recording an 18% revenue growth compared to the
previous year.

The most significant result was not just numerical. We successfully
completed the migration of 85% of our clients to the cloud
platform, a milestone that positions us as a leader in the Italian
SME segment for cloud-based management solutions.

Looking ahead, our Strategic Plan 2026-2028 is built on three
pillars: international expansion (DACH and Iberia markets),
AI integration into our products, and development of the training
channel as a second revenue stream.

The biggest challenge remains talent attraction and retention.
The 22% turnover in the Development department demands a serious
reflection on our value proposition as an employer. On this front,
the new "TechGrowth" program represents our concrete response.

I thank all employees, clients, and partners for the trust they
have placed in us. The best is yet to come.

Maria Conti
Chief Executive Officer

4. BUSINESS UNIT ANALYSIS
================================================================

4.1 GESTPRO CLOUD (63% of revenue)

The flagship product recorded 23.9% growth driven by the migration
of on-premise clients to the cloud and the acquisition of 380 net
new clients.

Key metrics:
- ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue): 8.9M euros (+24%)
- Active clients: 2,420 (+18%)
- Churn rate: 4.2% (down from 5.8% in 2024)
- NPS (Net Promoter Score): 52 (up from 45)
- ARPU (Average Revenue Per User): 3,696 euros/year (+5%)

The introduction of the AI module for automatic invoice
categorization (released in Q3) contributed to the reduction
in churn and the increase in NPS.

4.2 PREMIUM SUPPORT (19% of revenue)

Moderate growth (+12%) but with high margins (65%).

Key metrics:
- Premium clients: 540 (22% of client base)
- Average resolution time: 2.4 hours (target: 2 hours)
- Support satisfaction: 4.3/5.0
- Average monthly tickets: 1,200

Critical issue: the average resolution time is still above the
target. The plan includes the introduction of an AI chatbot for
first-level support by Q2 2026.

4.3 TECHACADEMY -- TRAINING (9% of revenue)

The training business unit shows the weakest growth (+6.1%).
Traditional classroom courses are declining (-15%), partially
offset by online course growth (+45%).

Courses delivered in 2025:
- Classroom courses: 48 (vs 62 in 2024)
- Live online courses: 85 (vs 52 in 2024)
- On-demand courses: 12 titles available (vs 5 in 2024)
- Total participants: 1,840 (+8%)

Plan: in 2026, the "TechAcademy Online" e-learning platform
will be launched with a subscription model (target: 200
subscribers in the first year, ARPU 1,200 euros/year).

4.4 PROFESSIONAL SERVICES (9% of revenue)

Consulting, customization, and integration. Growth of 6.3%
with stable margins at 40%.

Note: management is evaluating whether this BU should remain
standalone or be integrated as an ancillary service to the
other BUs. Decision expected by Q1 2026.

8. RISKS AND OPPORTUNITIES
================================================================

KEY RISKS

1. REVENUE CONCENTRATION
   15% of revenue depends on the top 10 clients.
   Mitigation: client base diversification, no single client
   above 3% of revenue.

2. TALENT RETENTION
   Turnover in the Development department (22%) threatens
   execution capability. Mitigation: TechGrowth program,
   compensation review, employer branding.

3. CYBERSECURITY
   The increased attack surface related to cloud requires
   continuous investment. Cybersecurity budget 2026: +25%.

4. AI REGULATION
   The EU AI Act will impose new requirements on products that
   integrate AI. Expected impact: compliance costs +150K euros
   in 2026.

5. COMPETITION
   Entry of international players (SAP Business One, Zoho)
   into the Italian SME segment. Differentiation: localization
   and Italian-language support.

OPPORTUNITIES

1. AI MARKET FOR SMEs
   72% of Italian SMEs do not yet use AI tools. TechSolutions
   is positioned to be the go-to partner.

2. INTERNATIONAL EXPANSION
   The DACH markets (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) and Iberia
   show growing demand for localized cloud ERP solutions.

3. TRAINING AS RECURRING REVENUE
   The TechAcademy Online platform can generate high-margin
   revenue with minimal incremental costs.

4. STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIPS
   Agreements with system integrators and accounting firms to
   expand the indirect sales channel.
