## Exercise 3: Self-Assessment Quiz (5 min)

Answer without consulting the slides. Mark the correct answer.

### Question 1
What does an LLM (Large Language Model) do?

- a) It searches for information on the Internet in real time
- b) It predicts the most likely next word
- c) It memorizes all the texts it was trained on
- d) It reasons like a human being

### Question 2
Which of the following statements is TRUE?

- a) ChatGPT is always updated to the latest hour
- b) An LLM can make up facts that sound true (hallucinations)
- c) Open-source models are always worse than commercial ones
- d) AI has consciousness and truly understands language

### Question 3
What is the "context window"?

- a) The browser window where you use ChatGPT
- b) The maximum amount of text the model can "see" within a conversation
- c) The time period during which the model was trained
- d) The graphical interface of the AI tool

### Question 4
Which statement about "temperature" is correct?

- a) High temperature = more precise and repeatable responses
- b) Low temperature = more creative and varied responses
- c) High temperature = more creative and varied responses
- d) Temperature does not affect the responses

### Question 5
What is the main difference between narrow AI and general AI?

- a) Narrow AI is free, general AI is paid
- b) Narrow AI is specialized in one task, general AI is capable of any intellectual task
- c) Narrow AI is old, general AI is new
- d) There is no difference

---

### Quiz Answers
1: b | 2: b | 3: b | 4: c | 5: b
